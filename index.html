Statistical Rationale & Framework Architecture

This application applies the core principles of statistical decision theory and cumulative probability models (CPM), inspired by the teachings of biometrician Frank Harrell (Biostatistics for Biomedical Research).

1. Multi-Criteria Utility $U(x)$ Instead of arbitrary scoring, options are evaluated via weighted utility functions combining ROI, time-to-transition, and work-life balance without loss of information.
2. Bayesian Credible Intervals Success probabilities incorporate user priors (background, programming skill, GCP certification) to calculate honest uncertainty bands rather than misleading point estimates.

By evaluating continuous metrics and avoiding artificial binary cutoffs (dichotomization), you gain a robust, transparent roadmap to guide major life and career transitions.
